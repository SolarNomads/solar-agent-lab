# E09. The @solar_list growth and trial loop

[Back](../README.md) · 29 Oct – 16 Nov 2026 (ongoing)

## Question
Can an agent team grow a niche Instagram account (temples and pilgrimage in India) organically, and can it learn *why* something works, rather than only posting more?

## What happened (measured)
- Followers: **297 on 29 Oct → 5,704 on 10 Nov → 7,070 on 15 Nov**, from the team's own logged snapshots. No ads (owner statement).
- Instagram Insights, 30-day window read on 11 Nov: about 1.2M views, 98.4% from non-followers. Our snapshots show under 10,000 views before 31 Oct, so nearly all of it came in about 12 days.
- Best single reel: about 676K views (our log, 10 Nov).

## How the team works on it
- **Studio roles** (scout, miner, writer, footage, animator, builder, critic, QC), with seats from [E01](E01-which-model-for-which-role.md).
- **Mining:** top creators' best-performing videos and their most-engaged comments are mined for ideas that have not become videos yet.
- **Measured design gates:** text-safe zones, card legibility (APCA), card–voice sync and motion QC. A piece that fails a gate is not delivered.
- **Creator lessons as playbooks:** for example, a 15 Nov study of a creator coach's caption styles became a rule: cinematic travel reels get minimal, small, low captions.
- **The metric:** sends per 1,000 views (target 10 or more), not likes.

## The trial loop (experiments on the account)
Instagram's "trial reels" are shown to non-followers first. We turned them into an experiment loop:
1. **A thesis card is logged before posting:** thesis, the one variable (cover, first 3 s, narration, music, lettering or length), a prediction, and the metric. Example: "the closing noun of the spoken hook changes retention: 'one walk' vs 'one hill' vs 'one road'".
2. **Only one variable changes;** everything else is identical, including the post time within ±15 minutes.
3. **Read at 24 h and 72 h, decide at 72 h.** A result is inconclusive unless both variants reach 1,000 views and the winner has at least 10 sends.

**Status, honestly:** the loop is designed and the posting path works, but **no trial has a decided outcome yet.** Most of the work so far went into making trials possible through the API:
- A first API post with the documented trial parameter came out as a normal public reel; Sol archived it.
- A small canary then confirmed the working recipe (11 Nov), and Sol confirmed it on his phone.
- Untitled canaries performed weakly, so "never post an untitled trial" became a rule.

The first equal-age comparisons (a hook change on a map reel, and a 24 s vs 41 s cut) are being read now.

## Lessons
- Platform documentation was silent or wrong on key details (trial eligibility, per-day limits, API behaviour). A tiny, cheap canary answered in an hour what reading could not.
- Growth came before understanding. We can show the account grew; we cannot yet say which choices caused it. The trial loop exists to close that gap.

## Limitations
One account, one niche, three weeks. Views and followers are platform numbers. No control account.
